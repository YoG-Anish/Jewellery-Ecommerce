CUSTOM BRIDAL
EARRINGS

Just for you, forever

With a true love and dedication to my craft, I love getting to know every bride I work with. Understanding a bride’s style and vision for their big day is integral to the design process, and after an initial consultation, the planning process begins. Creating mood boards and concept sketches using your ideas and specifications, the end result will be a unique design created just for you - kept forever as a beautiful memory of your wedding day.

designed-for-you earrings

how to order

  • 1 - Contact: Complete our enquiry form below
  • 2 - Ideas: Once the enquiry form has been received, you will be contacted directly and we can discuss any ideas, inspiration or styles you have in mind. I may ask you to send any photographs of your dress, headpiece or veil to help with the design process.
  • 3 - The design process begins: I will begin sketching, sculpting and sourcing. I will keep you updated throughout the whole process - sending options to choose from and ideas to review.
  • 4 - Say I do: Once we have an approved design, final photographs will be sent before they are wrapped, boxed and posted to you.

Remember… no idea is too crazy, too loud or too bland.
I am here to take your ideas and make them into something beautiful that you can keep forever.
