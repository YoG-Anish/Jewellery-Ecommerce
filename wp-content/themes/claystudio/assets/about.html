the gemini story

SINCE 2022

I’m Sophie, the proud owner and creative soul of Gemini Clay Studio.

My journey into jewellery making began in 2020, igniting my creative spark, making jewellery for myself. In 2022, I took the bold leap to transform my hobby into a business, Gemini Clay Studio, allowing me to share my designs and products with others.

I take inspiration from art movements, to vibrant to serene colours, and various fashion trends. My pieces are an inspired blend from the past, present, and future, reflecting my love for all things beautiful.

At Gemini Clay Studio, I believe in keeping products fresh and exciting. That’s why, along with our signature styles, new collections are added every 2-4 months. So, whether you’re looking for daring or delicate, there’s a style for you. Choose from our unique collections, or if you are seeking bespoke wedding jewellery that perfectly matches your bridal look, Gemini Clay Studio offers high quality for all.
